## Final prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: compact editorial illustration for a Georgian job search website, landscape 3:2. A carefully art-directed cut-paper collage of three everyday work objects: a cobalt blue delivery parcel with a small cyan folded flap, a pale blue desk lamp with an orange circular switch, and a white coffee cup on a cobalt saucer. Asymmetrical composition, large lamp on right, parcel lower left, cup upper left, slight overlaps with generous negative space. Sophisticated graphic illustration with flat cut-paper planes, subtle tactile paper grain, restrained minimal dimensional shadows, crisp bespoke silhouettes, warm off-white background #f8f7f3. Palette predominantly cobalt #2457e6, pale cyan, ink navy with only one tiny warm orange accent. Mature editorial magazine art, not childish, not glossy 3D clipart. Entire objects fit inside frame with generous edge room. NO people, hands, faces, lettering, logos, initials, X symbols, watermarks, badges, interface elements. Do not reference any previous architecture or logo scene. The image must work as a small supporting composition next to a large headline, not a full background.
